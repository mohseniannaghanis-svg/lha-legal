Privacy Policy

LHA Social Dashboard · last updated 14 October 2026

The dashboard only accesses accounts that the owner has connected via the official login of each platform. It reads profile information, follower counts and statistics of the owner's own posts, and posts content only when the owner schedules it.

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps).
